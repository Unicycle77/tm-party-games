Taskmaster · an app task

Prize task

Everyone brings a prize on tonight's theme and sends photos of it. You present each one on the TV, and the winner of the night takes the lot home.

At a glance

Who plays
Everyone, one prize at a time.
How long
About a minute a prize
Your prep
Pick the theme, e.g. the most unusual thing to bring on a first date.
To bring
Every guest brings a prize.
Winner
Your call for the task. The night's winner takes every prize.
1 · Before the party

Bring it. Photograph it.

Guests send photos of their prize from their phone, any time before it's presented, and star the one that stands for it.

2 · On the TV

The winner takes the lot.

At the end of the night the winner goes up in the middle of the TV with every prize hung around them.

3 · The host

Present, then score.

You pick whose prize goes up and step through their photos. The points rows sit right under it.

All you need is a theme and guests who take gift-giving too seriously.

Your time starts now.
