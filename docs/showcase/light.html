Taskmaster · an app task

The light

A computer with a webcam watches the room all night. Two seconds after anyone stops smiling, a light goes on. Guests have to work out why.

At a glance

Who plays
Everyone, all night.
How long
All evening, in the background
Your prep
Pick the secret, and set up a computer with a webcam facing the room.
To bring
A laptop with a webcam. A Hue lamp is optional.
Winner
First to work it out.
1 · The secret

Pick what sets it off.

Stops smiling, raises their eyebrows, touches their face: seven things the camera can spot. Only you know which one you picked.

2 · In the room

Or light them all.

Switch to All at once and every secret gets its own coloured bulb. Now the room has six mysteries going at once.

3 · The host

Tap whoever just got it.

Your phone shows what the camera sees and who's worked it out, in order. Their points follow their place.

All you need is a webcam and a room that can't stop smiling.

Your time starts now.
