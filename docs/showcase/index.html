Taskmaster · a party game

Your living room is the Taskmaster's house.

One TV for the stage. A phone for every contestant. A phone for the host.

1 · Walk in

Scan the TV. Find your name tag.

Every guest joins from their own phone, with nothing to download. Scan the QR on the TV, tap your name tag, and pick a sealed envelope from the pile.

Swipe for more →

2 · The tasks

Tasks straight out of the show.

The host picks the next task from their phone, and everyone's phone changes to play it. Here are three, starting with Noisemaster.

Noisemaster

Sixteen blank buttons on your phone, each one a sound on the TV. Press them in the right order to make it say the phrase.

  • Secret Tasks

    A sealed envelope on your phone. Do what it says without anyone catching you, until the Taskmaster asks who can keep a secret.

  • Prize task

    Everyone brings a prize. Each one is hung in the gold frame on the TV, and the winner takes the whole haul home.

3 · Run the show

The host holds the remote.

One phone runs the night: the order of tasks, who goes up on the TV next, and everyone's points. The gold button is always the next thing to press.

Swipe for more →

4 · The scoreboard

Scores go up on the TV.

When the host updates the scores, the wax seals tick up, everyone shuffles into order, and the leader's frame grows.

5 · The Prize Haul

The winner takes the lot.

At the end of the night the winner goes up in the middle, with every prize hung around them and a placard saying who brought it.

All you need is a TV, a phone each, and a prize to bring.

Your time starts now.
