Taskmaster · an app task

Photos

Write any photo task you like. Everyone sends one photo from their phone, and they go up on the TV one by one.

At a glance

Who plays
Everyone at once.
How long
A few minutes to take them, about a minute each to show
Your prep
Write the task, e.g. the best costume in the room.
To bring
Nothing.
Winner
Your call: you give the points.
1 · The task

One photo each. No take-backs.

Tonight's task: “Take a photo of the best costume in the room that isn't yours.” Everyone takes it on their phone and sends it in. Once it's sent, it's locked.

2 · On the TV

Then everyone's, side by side.

Show them one at a time for the story behind each, then all at once so the room can compare.

3 · The host

One tap per photo.

Your phone lists who's sent theirs. Tap a name and their photo goes up. The points are right there underneath.

Use it as often as you like: every photo task you can think of is one more Photos.

Your time starts now.
