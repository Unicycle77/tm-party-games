Secret Tasks

Every guest picks a sealed envelope when they arrive. Inside is a secret task to do all night without getting caught.

At a glance

Who plays
Everyone, all night.
How long
All evening, then about a minute each at the reveal
Your prep
Nothing. The pile has secrets built in; add your own if you like.
To bring
Nothing.
Winner
Your call at the reveal.
1 · Check in

Pick an envelope. Tell no one.

At check-in, every guest drags an envelope off the pile and breaks the seal. They can open it again any time to check, then seal it back up.

2 · On the TV

Who can keep a secret?

At the end, each envelope opens on the TV. The room says whether they ever caught them at it.

3 · The host

You know everyone's secret.

Your phone shows who has which task and who hasn't looked yet, so you can nudge them.

All you need is a pile of envelopes and guests who can keep a straight face.

Your time starts now.
