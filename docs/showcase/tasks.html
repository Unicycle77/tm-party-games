Taskmaster · the app tasks

Pick your tasks.

Nine tasks the app runs for you. A good night mixes a few from each kind.

On the spot

One or two guests take a turn while everyone else watches the TV.

  • Noisemaster

    Sixteen blank buttons. Make them say the phrase.

  • [BLANK] in a Box

    One peeks. One decides: swap or keep?

Everyone at once

Every guest plays at the same time, then the TV shows what they made.

  • Photos

    Everyone sends in one photo.

  • Before & After

    Everyone films one video, from before to after.

  • Flipchart

    Sixteen pages, one portrait. Draw it a page at a time.

  • Headshot

    Everyone's most confident headshot, judged.

  • Prize task

    Everyone's prize, presented one by one.

All night, in the background

It starts when guests arrive and runs under everything else, until the host calls it.

  • Secret Tasks

    A sealed envelope on your phone. Tell no one.

  • The light

    A light that goes on for a reason. Work out why.

Then add them to Plan the party, in any order you like.
