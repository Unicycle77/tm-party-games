Taskmaster · an app task

[BLANK] in a Box

Two guests, two boxes, and one of them knows where the eggplant is. The other has to decide whether to swap.

At a glance

Who plays
Two guests at a time. Everyone else watches the TV.
How long
About 2 minutes a pair
Your prep
Nothing. Pick who plays and what's in the box.
To bring
Nothing. The boxes and what's in them are in the app.
Winner
Whoever ends up with the eggplant.
1 · The turn

One peeks. One decides.

Greg holds a button to look inside his box, as often as he likes. Alex can't look. He can only watch Greg's face, ask questions, and then lock in swap or keep.

2 · On the TV

The lids come off.

The host opens the boxes on the big screen. Alex swapped, and the eggplant was in Greg's box all along.

3 · The host

Pick two. Open the boxes.

You pick the two guests and the object. While they play, your phone says who looked and waits for the choice. Then you open each box when the room is ready.

All you need is two guests and one of them with a good poker face.

Your time starts now.
